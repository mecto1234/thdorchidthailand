PRICING

Wholesale pricing is quoted per order.

The quotation can vary according to four practical factors.

01

Product

Lei style, loose blooms, and cut stems have different material and preparation requirements.

02

Quantity

Order volume affects flower supply, labor, packing, and the quotation.

03

Season

High-demand periods such as graduation season can affect supply and price.

04

Availability

Final availability is confirmed before the order is accepted.

FREIGHT & COMMERCIAL TERMS

Freight responsibility is negotiable.

The customer and THD Orchid can discuss who will pay the air-freight cost and how the shipment will be arranged. There is no single freight arrangement that must apply to every order; the final deal is agreed case by case.

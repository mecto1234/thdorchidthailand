WHO WE ARE

We are not only selling flowers. We manage the journey behind them.

Our own orchid farm gives us a direct connection to the flowers we use. From there, our work continues through selection, lei making, loose-bloom preparation, post-harvest handling, packing, and shipment coordination.

For Hawaii, orchid lei and loose orchid blooms are core product categories. Fresh cut orchid stems are also available as an additional offering.

When order demand exceeds our own production, additional flowers may be sourced from outside growers. Availability and quality are reviewed as part of each wholesale order.

INSIDE THD ORCHID

The operation behind the product.

TECHNICAL KNOW-HOW

Freshness is managed through process, not just appearance.

Our export operation includes specialized handling steps that support product quality and Hawaii-bound preparation. Exact treatment and shipment requirements are confirmed for the applicable product and order.

01

PEST-CONTROL PREPARATION

Methyl Bromide

Methyl Bromide fumigation is part of our current Hawaii-bound lei preparation workflow where applicable.

02

POST-HARVEST HANDLING

1-MCP Treatment

1-MCP is used as part of our post-harvest quality management where appropriate, helping reduce ethylene response during handling.

03

TEMPERATURE MANAGEMENT

Cold Handling

Cold-room handling and temperature management are integrated into our pre-shipment workflow for fresh orchid products.

04

EXPORT PREPARATION

Packing & Cargo Readiness

Orders are packed with product protection, handling, and air-cargo movement in mind before handover to the shipping agent.
